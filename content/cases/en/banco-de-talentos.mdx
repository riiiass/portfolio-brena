<CaseSection phase="definir" title="Approach">

- A site in **vanilla HTML, CSS and JavaScript**, no framework: lightweight and easy to maintain.
- Design inspired by **Cosmos**, with each person's work front and center.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection phase="desenvolver" title="Process">

- A hero with the line **“Find who is a Baker”**, with real photos of their work floating around it.
- **Search by trade, name or product** at the top and in the middle of the page.
- A band with the trades (entrepreneur, baker, artisan, manicurist) running in a loop.
- **“My selection”**: visitors save the people they liked.
