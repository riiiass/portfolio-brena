<CaseSection phase="descobrir" title="Challenge">

Instituto Paz no Lar is dedicated to **supporting women** who have been through hard situations such as abuse and other forms of violence. The nonprofit needed two things in one place:

- **A public website** presenting its work: home, the organization's story, photos, contact details and Instagram, in a way that draws people in and gets them interested.
- **A staff-only area** to register and maintain information about everyone in the program — women supported, employees, board members and volunteers — and use it as an organized repository, with **printing and PDF export**.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection phase="definir" title="Approach">

I volunteer on the project, handling **development and design**.

- **Next.js (React)** for the public site and the admin area in the same project.
- **Prisma** with **PostgreSQL on Neon**, in the **São Paulo region**, close to its users.
- Code on **GitHub** and deployment on **Netlify**.
- Because the internal area stores personal and sensitive information, access is **limited to authenticated staff** and no internal screen appears in this portfolio.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection phase="desenvolver" title="Process">

### Public website

Designed for people arriving in search of help or wanting to support the nonprofit:

- A large, direct title, **“Paz no Lar”** (Peace at Home), with the organization's name right above it.
- Two clear actions: **“Learn about the benefits”** (primary) and **“Contact us”** (secondary).
- Trust badges right below: **free support, confidentiality and respect, dedicated team**.
- Sections for the **story**, photos, contact and Instagram.

### Staff area

- **Login** set apart in the corner, labeled “staff only”.
- **Create and edit** records for women supported, employees, board members and volunteers.
- The information becomes the nonprofit's **repository**, ready to **print or export as PDF**.

</CaseSection>
