<CaseSection phase="descobrir" title="Challenge">

KADOSH is a faith-inspired fashion brand for men and women. The brief was a **complete website** where customers do everything on their own, without having to talk to the company:

- A **dynamic** showcase that presents the shirts in a way that stands out.
- Sales for **retail and wholesale** on the same site.
- **Shipping calculated** during checkout.
- **Payment on the site itself**.
- An **admin area** for the brand to manage products, stock and orders.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection phase="definir" title="Approach">

I chose a foundation that could handle the storefront and the business rules in the same project:

- **Next.js 15 (App Router) with React 19 and TypeScript** for both the interface and the APIs, typed end to end.
- **PostgreSQL on Neon with Prisma** to model products, variants, inventory, orders and payments.
- **Zod** validating every piece of data that reaches the server.
- Editorial identity: **Cormorant Garamond** for headings and **Jost** for body text, black and gold.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection phase="desenvolver" title="Process">

### Storefront and navigation

Animations with **Framer Motion**: a rotating showcase, horizontal product scrolling, a side menu and a side cart. Icons by Lucide.

### Checkout, shipping and inventory

- **PagBank (Checkout)**: Pix and credit card, with payment confirmation through **webhooks**.
- **SuperFrete**: shipping quotes with Correios, Jadlog and Loggi.
- **ViaCEP**: the address fills itself in from the postal code.
- **Stock reservation** during checkout, with a Netlify scheduled function that releases expired reservations.
- **Resend** for transactional emails, when configured.

### Admin panel

Login built into the project, with no external library: **scrypt password hashing** and an **HMAC-signed session** in a secure cookie. Panel actions use Server Actions.

### Quality

Automated tests with **Vitest** (wholesale rules, CPF validation, webhook signatures, shipping and session) running on **GitHub Actions** on every push.

</CaseSection>
